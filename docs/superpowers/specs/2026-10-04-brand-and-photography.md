# Detoks. Brand, photography and the complete customer journey

## Mandate and execution

The owner delegated the complete process on 4 October 2026, including all website content and naming/integration of taha-foto-kucuk.zip. Continue the existing review branch. Preserve main and closed payments. The goal is a distinctive Taha-led shop and a repeatable operating model for Western Thrace small businesses, not a generic wellness store. Do not wait for approval between reversible implementation steps.

## Design choice

Evolve the existing TanStack storefront. A new platform or an unrelated template would discard tested commerce boundaries. Use a warm paper shopping surface, walnut navigation and footer, restrained copper accents, Syne and Figtree. Actual supplied product-study photography leads the home page. No fictional shop interiors or portrait substitutes.

Brand premise, in Turkish, is “Bir dükkânın özeni. Her güne bir parça.” It describes the relationship between a known shopkeeper, a considered selection and ordinary life, not a treatment or shipping promise. Founder biography uses only the known Komotini shop and previously supplied physiotherapy background. No invented year, family lineage, quote, accreditation or manufacturing method. The two shelves remain visible and navigable.

## Photography contract

151 source JPEGs, each identified by its original MF8A filename and SHA256. All receive neutral, visually supported names and optimized WebP derivatives. Original bytes remain unchanged outside the source repository. Strip EXIF metadata from web derivatives. Never infer a medicinal plant species or proprietary blend from appearance. Visually recognizable subject names may be used as descriptions, not verified SKU identities. Every asset records subject, group, editorial status, source name and variants. A separate owner-approved map is the only route to exact product-photo status. All photos are accessible through the photographic shelf, 24 at a time.

## Information architecture

Home, catalogue, category, product, story, photographic shelf, journal and three editorial notes, contact, gift inquiry, trade inquiry, delivery and legal/privacy hub. Navigation and page endings form complete onward journeys. Gift and trade inquiries open user-controlled WhatsApp messages containing their selections. No fake submissions, stock, prices, response guarantees or personal-data persistence.

## Language and content contract

Every new page and interactive label has TR, EL, EN and the remaining 17 supported locales. Complete key parity, no hidden English fallback for a newly written page. Primary-language copy is written as complete paragraphs. Locale changes preserve query state. Legacy keyword substitutions are not represented as verified translations. Do not promise all 27 EU countries are already served. The customer-facing ambition and operating readiness are distinct.

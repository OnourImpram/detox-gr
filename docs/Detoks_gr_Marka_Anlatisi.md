### EN uyarlama notları (5)
1. "Aktar" çevrilmez: ilk geçişte "herbalist's shop", sonra "the shop". "Apothecary" (tıp çağrışımı) ve "wellness shop" (iddia çağrışımı) yasak.
2. Yer adları: Komotini, Rhodope; "Western Thrace" bağlam için yalnız ilk geçişte bir kez.
3. Cümle disiplini aynı: 12 kelime üst sınırı EN'de de geçerli; sıcaklık "the" ile değil nesneyle kurulur: jar, paper, label, spoon.
4. "List" kalıbı korunur: "Add to list" doğal durur; "cart"a dönülmez. "Checkout" teknik terim olarak kalabilir.
5. İddia dili EN'de de kapalı: "natural", "healthy", "pure" toplu kullanılmaz (AB 1924/2006 + ödeme sağlayıcı riski); içerik yazılır, nitelik yazılmaz. "Detox" yalnız marka adı.

### EL uyarlama notları (5)
1. Marka «Detoks» olarak kalır; «αποτοξίνωση» vaat olarak asla. "Aktar" karşılığı: «μαγαζί με βότανα», kısada «το μαγαζί». «Μπακάλικο» demeyin — yalnız gıda çağrıştırır, sabunu ve kozmetiği dışarıda bırakır.

5. Yer adları yerel yazım: «Κομοτηνή», «Ροδόπη», «Μπιζάνιου 14». Türkçe konuşan yerel okur için adres satırında Latin karşılık da dursun.

*Title: Some stories do not start with a door. They start with curiosity.*

Some stories do not start when a shop door opens. They start with the curiosity of the person who will open it: a hand reaching for a jar, an eye reading a label, a voice asking "what is this?". This story started that way.

Before plants, Taha Hüseyinoğlu worked with the body. Physiotherapy was his work: listening to a knee, a shoulder, a back; explaining a movement step by step. The curiosity comes from there. In time the same curiosity reached shelves, jars and plants — and became Detoks, in Komotini.

The shop is at Mpizaniou 14. The door opens at 09:30 in the morning; on Tuesdays and Thursdays it opens a second time at 18:00. Walk in and the smell reaches you first: spice, soap, dried herbs. Shelves hold jars, bottles and parcels; every label can be read.

The shop keeps two shelves. On one, what carries the Detoks Aktar label: vinegar, tarhana — what Taha prepares himself. On the other, makers he trusts: Biagros, Creta Carob and others, under their own names. He does not make everything, and he does not mind saying so. If a product says Detoks Aktar, he prepared it. If it does not, the maker's name is on the label. That is all.

Rhodope is not only an address. It is where Taha reads an ingredient: its mountain, its market, its garden. Knowing where a product comes from is knowing how to treat it.

The rule in this shop is simple: we write what we know and leave blank what we do not. We do not invent the weight of a product whose label does not state it. We do not write Rhodope on a product that is not from Rhodope. If you ask and there is no answer, he says "I don't know" — and writes it down once he learns.

Nothing on these shelves carries a grand promise. Vinegar goes into a salad, tarhana into the pot, lokum beside the coffee, soap by the sink, rose water in front of the morning mirror. The care we want to tell is not a grand change; it is a spoon added to breakfast.

So that those who cannot come are not left far away, he tells the recipes and the shelves on Instagram: @detoks_taha. Questions arrive there, answers leave from there; sometimes a message works as well as walking in.

Parcels leave this shop. Products are wrapped in paper, the label stuck on so you can read it; they travel from Komotini to a door in Europe.

Our story took root here. You decide where it joins yours.

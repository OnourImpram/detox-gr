# Detoks v4. Botanical editorial storefront

## User direction

Onour asked for a visual redesign, specifically grounded in his previously supplied design repositories and rules. The current v3.2 interface works but its walnut-brown header, uniform sans-serif type, repeated eyebrows and even card rows do not meet his design expectations. Previous instructions delegate the site implementation and integration to the assistant. Preserve commercial truth, the 151 real photographs, all 30 generated compositions, product routes and the existing interactions.

## Proposed visual world

An independent Komotini provisioner, not a wellness SaaS template. Warm mineral paper, dark olive ink, a restrained clay secondary accent. Literata for titles and product names, Figtree for interface and body copy. Use the same palette on navigation, forms, product pages and footer rather than alternating unrelated light/dark sections. No neon, glassmorphism, gradient text, fabricated stats, fake testimonials, decorative 3D or invented shop/owner photos.

## Composition

A distinctive asymmetric hero with large editorial typography and a large photograph from the owner's archive. A small interactive botanical index changes the actual photograph. No autoplay or scroll hijacking. Open product cards with carefully aligned purchase controls. The two shelves become a real editorial spread rather than two matching icon cards. Category imagery has a deliberate unequal rhythm. Gift, founder story and journal each have their own layout. A calm, large typographic footer completes the same visual world.

Catalogue and PDP remain efficient operational surfaces. Preserve quick add, filters, list mode, multiword search, keyboard zoom, selected country and inquiry alternatives. Product photos retain their exact provenance labels. Typography, form states, modal focus, 320px layouts and long translated text are acceptance criteria, not optional polish.

## References actually reviewed

* pbakaus/impeccable, README and skill/reference/craft-floor.md. Distinct product world, actual computed contrast, no repeated eyebrow above every heading, no decorative statistic scaffold, custom display typography, live visual iteration.
* Leonxlnx/taste-skill, README and skills/redesign-skill/SKILL.md. Existing-stack redesign, serif/sans contrast, asymmetry, spacing, state preservation. Its suggestions to invent data or randomize dates are explicitly rejected here.
* nexu-io/open-design, README. Keep PRODUCT truth separate from a durable DESIGN.md and inspect the implemented result.
* dickwu/apple-design-skill, README. Clear hierarchy, usable controls, platform-appropriate focus, adaptation and feedback. This is not an Apple-looking website.
* nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill, .claude/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md. 44px controls, 16px form inputs, semantic tokens, reduced motion and responsive/contrast checks.
* brijr/iris, README. Inspect real rendered screens. Existing Playwright/Chromium supplies the same screenshot task, without installing another runtime.
* bergside/awesome-design-skills, README. Separate reusable token decisions and human-readable intent. No template copied.
* img2threejs/img2threejs, README. Reviewed, but procedural product reconstruction is inappropriate without verified geometry and not used.
* aladicf/better-web-ui, README. Contextual hierarchy and deliberate surface differences, preserving the TanStack stack.

No external installer, model API or analytics is introduced. Source references guide design; they do not override product truth or authorize operations.
